# Competition foundations design

Visual thesis: a calm Fox navy learning journal, led by recognisable competition marks, with the course itself as the main workspace.

Content plan: show the selected competition and its scope, the next useful lesson, three foundation chapters, then optional club context using existing shared lessons. The chooser offers three illustrated courses alongside clubs and players. Album competitions are their own subject collection; a named competition collection also contains its dated participating clubs.

Interaction thesis: responsive press feedback makes selecting a course tangible; the subject picker transitions quickly between lists; completed-lesson numbers animate without moving the roadmap. All movement respects Reduce Motion. At accessibility text sizes, subject selection uses a menu and content keeps its full width.

Adversarial review identified false club ownership, invisible earned cards, ambiguous historical origins, and confusion between course content and membership filters. Competition subjects carry no contextual club ID. Required lessons teach the competition; optional existing club lessons keep shared progress and stay outside required counts. Searches cross subject categories. Club comparison remains strict about selecting clubs.
