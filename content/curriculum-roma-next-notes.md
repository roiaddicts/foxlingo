## Scope and caveats

The club’s 2019 announcement gives De Rossi’s appearance total as 615 “to date” before his final Roma match; the later retirement account gives the completed 616. This pack uses the completed total. Roma’s current history page calls 2000-01 the third Scudetto, while the 1982-83 article labels the 1983 title as the second. Totti’s final totals are the official Hall of Fame table totals, rather than an evolving current statistic. The 1984 match score is sourced to UEFA’s season archive; the club history itself confirms that the final reached penalties but does not state the score in the opened text. The two score-choice exercises require canonical club badges already present for Roma, Liverpool and Barcelona. No other score facts were added.

AS Roma pages include cookie notices for some third-party embedded media, but the historical text used here was visible in the opened pages. No claims rely on embedded media. The pack was not registered or compiled.
