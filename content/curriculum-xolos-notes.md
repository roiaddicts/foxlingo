# Club Tijuana player and moment curriculum

Verified 2026-10-10. This pack adds 7 lessons: one new Club Tijuana defining-moment lesson and two new lessons for each existing player entity (six player lessons, three facts each). The player lessons enrich the existing catalog identities; `players` is intentionally empty so it does not redefine them.

## Existing identities and scope

The source catalog already defines `entity-xolos` for Club Tijuana, plus `entity-riascos` (Duvier Riascos), `entity-saucedo` (Cirilo Saucedo), and `entity-arce` (Fernando Arce). This pack uses those exact subject keys and adds no player records, portrait URLs, or seed bindings. The game seed file does not contain these legacy Xolos players; their stable starter-catalog entities and assets remain the identity source.

Existing lesson semantics are preserved. New facts avoid the starter anchors and earlier enrichment: Riascos’s country/position/Puebla move, 2005 professional debut, 2010 China scoring title and 2019 retrospective total; Saucedo’s position, 11 goals conceded in 17 Clausura 2012 regular matches, Pan American bronze, Mexico debut year and arrival from Tigres; Arce’s position, Tijuana origin, Apertura 2012 club, Irapuato debut, Veracruz transfer and Mexico debut year. The club moment is the Apertura 2012 semifinal comeback against León, not the already-covered final against Toluca.

## Source and coverage notes

All new claims come from opened primary Club Tijuana match reports and official retrospective/profile material. The 2012 Morelia report is dated October 27 and bounds Riascos’s two-campaign 12-goal count and his seventh goal of that Apertura to that date; neither is presented as a current total. The 2012 Saucedo article distinguishes 17 regular-phase games from 19 appearances including the quarterfinal series; the 11-goal fact already exists in the older lesson and is not repeated here. The added Saucedo facts are the least-goals-conceded distinction, eight shutouts in 19 appearances, and the award shortlist, followed by a separate 2013 first-captaincy milestone.

Arce’s 401st Primera División appearance and 12th-minute free kick are scoped to the July 20, 2012 Apertura opener against Puebla. His separate Santos lesson refers to Club Tijuana’s first top-flight victory on August 6, 2011, not a later win or the 2012 final. The club’s official historical match table supplies the date, 3–1 score and Arce goal at 43 minutes.

The Xolos club moment is the November 25, 2012 Apertura semifinal return: 3–0 over León, 3–2 aggregate after a 0–2 first leg. It only covers qualification for the final and does not repeat the final’s opponent, aggregate or title date. All dates and career totals in the new fact rows carry explicit season/competition scope. No missing sources or unresolved identity ambiguities remain for these seven lessons.
