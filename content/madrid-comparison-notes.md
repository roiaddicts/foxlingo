# Madrid appearances comparison draft

Verified on 2026-10-08 against the three official Real Madrid legend profiles. Each profile provides the player's Madrid career span and an all-tenure appearance total: Cristiano Ronaldo, 2009–2018, 438 competitive appearances; Sergio Ramos, 2005–2021, 671 appearances; Iker Casillas, 1999–2015, 725 official matches. The suggested ascending order is Ronaldo (438), Ramos (671), Casillas (725).

## Metric and scope

All three questions concern the total official first-team match appearances credited by Real Madrid over each player's complete Madrid tenure. The profiles do not use one perfectly uniform label: Ronaldo's profile says “competitive appearances,” Ramos's says “appearances,” and Casillas's says “official games.” The club's all-time appearance ranking page independently lists Ramos (671) and Casillas (725) under its Real Madrid games-played ranking. For product wording, retain “according to Real Madrid’s official profile” and treat the figures as club-reported competitive/official career totals; do not present them as season averages or normalize for tenure length. The career periods differ, so this is a count comparison, not a claim that the players are directly comparable across eras.

## Discrepancy note

Cristiano Ronaldo's goal total is reported inconsistently across sources: Real Madrid's official profile gives 451 goals in 438 competitive appearances, while widely repeated external totals use 450 goals. This draft compares appearances only; it does not rely on or adjudicate the goal-count discrepancy. The 438 appearance figure itself is the club profile's stated total.

## Retrieval and limitations

The official profile URLs were opened and checked on the verification date. The web renderer returned the page title but not a full visible body for these profile routes; the indexed official-page content exposed the stated tenure and statistics. The club's all-time ranking page was also checked as a cross-reference for Ramos and Casillas. No independent non-club appearance database was substituted. The lesson is a draft and should be independently rechecked before production use.
