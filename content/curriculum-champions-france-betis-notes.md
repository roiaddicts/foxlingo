## Research limits and handling

- The LOSC scoreline fact is Lens 0–3 Lille, with left/right score input matching Lens and Lille badges. Ligue 1’s derby recap says the result assured the league title with three games remaining. Betis has two separate score exercises from the 2022 final: 1–1 after regulation, then 5–4 in the shootout. Their prompts and scopes distinguish match score from shootout score.
- LOSC’s 2010–11 honours page reports 68 goals and four defeats in the season retrospective but does not state the competition basis. Both facts explicitly preserve that wording rather than label the values as all-competition totals.
- Joaquín’s 521 Betis appearances and 66 goals were his figures at the 19 April 2023 retirement announcement, before the season ended. The separate 622 LaLiga appearances are the completed total in the final-match report.
- The oversized LOSC Hazard retrospective was not used. Opened farewell and awards pages support the Hazard facts. The farewell report provides his 2011–12 Ligue 1 goals in a season-by-season table; the pack uses the league total, not an all-competition figure.
- Player seed artwork comes from exact reviewed catalog identities. Seko Fofana’s seed has no image URL, so the curriculum uses an initials fallback. Club badges are existing cached art. No official portrait source is needed for players with no seed artwork URL.
