## Research notes and limitations

- For Di Stéfano, the newly added moment is his debut in a 1953 friendly; his legacy story moves to the inaugural 1960 Intercontinental Cup. The new Madrid club defining moment is the 2000 final, not the 1950s European Cup run.
- Cristiano Ronaldo’s new record lesson uses four Ballon d’Or awards and six Champions League top-scorer finishes from the official profile; it avoids repeating the existing career totals of 451 goals, 105 Champions League goals, and four Champions League titles.
- Ramos’s 2018 Club World Cup final and his individual award selections are scoped to the described tournament/profile, not live totals.
- Casillas’s 2002 final facts distinguish his substitute appearance from his existing 1999 debut, appearance total, honours and World Cup question.
- Benzema’s Nations League goal is documented by UEFA; its score and opponent are the 10 October 2021 final, not an ongoing national-team statistic.
- Bale’s Club World Cup hat-trick is from the 2018 semi-final. His recorded 19 trophies and final award are completed-career facts from the farewell notice.
- Roberto Carlos’s 2002 international story identifies the Brazilian world champion and the final score; FIFA’s tournament history names him among the players in Brazil’s 2002 World Cup-winning side and records the final result.
