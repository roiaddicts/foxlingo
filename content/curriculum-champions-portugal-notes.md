## Evidence map

### A club begins by the Douro (`history`)
- `portugal-porto-founded-date` — In which year does UEFA list FC Porto as formed? **Answer:** 1893. UEFA lists FC Porto as formed in 1893. Source: `portugal-porto-facts`.
- `portugal-porto-nickname` — What nickname does UEFA give FC Porto? **Answer:** Dragões (The Dragons). UEFA lists the nickname Dragões (The Dragons). Source: `portugal-porto-facts`.
- `portugal-porto-record-win` — What score was Porto’s biggest home win in UEFA club competition, against Rabat Ajax in 1986? **Answer:** 9–0. UEFA records the 9–0 home win over Rabat Ajax on 17 September 1986. Source: `portugal-porto-facts`.

### The back-heel that changed Porto (`moments`)
- `portugal-porto-87-equaliser` — Who scored Porto’s equaliser against Bayern in the 1987 European Cup final? **Answer:** Rabah Madjer. UEFA says Madjer back-heeled past Jean-Marie Pfaff with time running out. Source: `portugal-porto-87`.
- `portugal-porto-87-technique` — How did Madjer beat Bayern goalkeeper Jean-Marie Pfaff for Porto’s equaliser? **Answer:** A back-heel. UEFA describes his equaliser as a back-heel beyond Pfaff. Source: `portugal-porto-87`.
- `portugal-porto-87-winner` — Who did Madjer set up four minutes after his equaliser? **Answer:** Juary. UEFA reports Madjer set up Filho Juary four minutes after his goal. Source: `portugal-porto-87`.

### Derlei decides it in Seville (`moments`)
- `portugal-porto-03-score` — What was the score when Porto beat Celtic in the 2003 UEFA Cup final after extra time? **Answer:** 3–2. UEFA reports Celtic 2–3 Porto after extra time. Source: `portugal-porto-03`.
- `portugal-porto-03-derlei-minute` — In which minute did Derlei score the extra-time winner against Celtic? **Answer:** 115th minute. UEFA states Derlei scored after 115 minutes. Source: `portugal-porto-03`.
- `portugal-porto-03-rule` — Under which unusual rule was the 2003 final decided? **Answer:** Silver goal. UEFA calls Porto the first team to win a trophy under the silver-goal rule. Source: `portugal-porto-03`.
