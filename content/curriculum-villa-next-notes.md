# Aston Villa curriculum research notes

Authored 2026-10-11. This unregistered pack contains 11 lessons and 33 facts: three club lessons and four personal lessons each for Paul McGrath and Dwight Yorke. Yorke joins to the exact `player-457` / `Dwight Yorke` seed identity in `games-en.json`; McGrath has no exact matching seed, so no player identity join or portrait was guessed. Club-score inputs use canonical home/left-to-right club order in metadata and score-only answers.

## Paul McGrath

- `villa-next-stpats-profile` — [St Patrick’s Athletic player profile](https://www.stpatsfc.com/profile.php?id=195). Supports `villa-mcgrath-first-club` (signed from Dalkey United in 1981) and `villa-mcgrath-next-club` (transferred to Manchester United after the 1981–82 season); the profile also records a later Aston Villa move. This primary club profile does not cover every later club in McGrath’s career, so no Sheffield United claim is included.
- `villa-next-derby-mcgrath` — [Derby County: The Jim Smith Era](https://www.dcfc.co.uk/news/2025/06/feature-the-jim-smith-era-a-premiership-legacy-june-2025). Supports `villa-mcgrath-derby-arrival`: Derby says McGrath arrived from Aston Villa in October 1996.
- `villa-next-stpats-history` — [St Pat’s History: The 1980s](https://www.stpatsfc.com/news.php?id=8802). Supports `villa-mcgrath-pfai-award`: the club identifies McGrath as the 1981–82 PFAI Player of the Year.
- `villa-next-fai-mcgrath` — [McGrath and O’Brien inducted into FAI Hall of Fame](https://www.stpatsfc.com/news.php?id=8749). Supports `villa-mcgrath-ireland-caps` (83 senior caps) and `villa-mcgrath-hall-of-fame` (2019 FAI Hall of Fame induction). It also confirms appearances at Euro 1988 and the 1990 and 1994 World Cups.
- `villa-next-uefa-mcgrath-worldcups` — [UEFA: Italy and Ireland meet 18 years on](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuro/history/news/0254-0d7da204b332-f70175e08ec7-1000--italy-and-ireland-meet-18-years-on/). Its match accounts and line-ups support `villa-mcgrath-1990-appearance` (McGrath started for Ireland), `villa-mcgrath-1990-stage` (quarter-final), and `villa-mcgrath-1990-goalscorer` (Salvatore Schillaci scored the only goal in Italy’s 1–0 win).
- `villa-next-fifa-mcgrath-italy` — [FIFA: Paul McGrath’s masterclass, World Cup 1994](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/articles/paul-mcgrath-performance-usa-1994). Supports `villa-mcgrath-italy-score` (Ireland beat Italy 1–0), `villa-mcgrath-italy-headers` (15 headers won), and `villa-mcgrath-italy-interceptions` (five interceptions). These are match-specific personal performance details.

The 1993 PFA Players’ Player of the Year claim was not found in an opened primary source, so it remains excluded. Claims that McGrath personally won Villa’s 1994 or 1996 League Cup finals were removed because the Villa timeline establishes team results but does not identify his participation in those finals.

## Dwight Yorke

- `villa-next-yorke-profile` — [Premier League player profile](https://www.premierleague.com/players/59/Dwight-Yorke/overview). This is the corrected official route for Dwight Yorke, rather than the previously mistaken Julian Joachim slug. Its season table supports `villa-yorke-first-17`: 17 Premier League goals for Villa in 1995/96.
- `villa-next-yorke-three-seasons` — [Aston Villa: Preview at Molineux](https://www.avfc.co.uk/news/2025/february/01/preview--a-trip-across-the-midlands-to-molineux/). Supports `villa-yorke-three-seasons`: the club identifies Yorke among the players with at least 15 Premier League goal involvements in each of three consecutive seasons, 1995/96 through 1997/98.
- `villa-next-efl-previous-finals` — [EFL Carabao Cup previous finals](https://www.efl.com/competitions/carabao-cup/previous-finals/). Supports `villa-yorke-1996-final-score` (Aston Villa 3–0 Leeds United), `villa-yorke-1996-goal-minute` (Yorke scored Villa’s third in the 88th minute), and `villa-yorke-1996-competition` (League Cup final). This source verifies Yorke’s personal scoring contribution, not only the club’s trophy.
- `villa-next-yorke-manutd-profile` — [Manchester United: When Dwight Yorke signed for United](https://www.manutd.com/en/amp/news/detail/20-years-since-dwight-yorke-signed-for-man-united). Supports `villa-yorke-champions-league-goals` (eight goals in 11 Champions League appearances during 1998/99) and the 18-goal Premier League campaign with a shared Golden Boot.
- `villa-next-yorke-united-treble` — [Premier League: Team spirit inspired Man Utd treble](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/59576). Supports `villa-yorke-move-to-united` (Yorke joined Manchester United from Villa in August 1998) and `villa-yorke-treble-total` (29 goals in all competitions during United’s treble-winning season).
- `villa-next-fifa-yorke-captain` — [FIFA: England–Trinidad and Tobago, 2006 World Cup](https://collect.fifa.com/marketplace/sog-d-08). Supports `villa-yorke-world-cup-country`: FIFA identifies Yorke as Trinidad and Tobago’s captain in the 2006 tournament.
- `villa-next-fifa-yorke-2006` — [FIFA: Hart on Trinidad and Tobago](https://inside.fifa.com/tournaments/mens/worldcup/2018russia/news/hart-make-the-t-t-players-feel-wanted-2856671). Supports `villa-yorke-first-world-cup` (the team’s only World Cup appearance at the time was Germany 2006) and `villa-yorke-sweden-result` (the opening match against Sweden ended 0–0).

The frequently repeated story that Graham Taylor discovered Yorke on a 1989 Caribbean tour was omitted because no opened primary source for the anecdote was located. Yorke’s 1995/96 17-goal fact appears only once. The earlier trivial prompt asking which club he represented across a long Premier League span was replaced with his documented 1998 move from Aston Villa to Manchester United.
