Seed identities were checked in `Foxlingo/Resources/games-en.json`: Thierry Henry is `player-80` / `Thierry Henry`; Dennis Bergkamp is `player-839` / `Dennis Bergkamp` (the `classic-player-*` records are separate duplicate portrait entries and are not used).

## Primary sources and scopes

- Arsenal's [Henry feature](https://arsenal-fc.shorthandstories.com/henry-on-goals-glory-and-the-power-of-our-cannon/index.html) says his completed Arsenal career ended at 228 goals from 377 appearances. This is the whole-club, all-competition total across both spells.
- The [Premier League Hall of Fame feature on Henry](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/2096418) gives 175 goals in 258 Premier League matches and notes that Henry came to Arsenal from Juventus in 1999. The [Premier League anniversary article](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/694184) says he returned on loan in 2012.
- FIFA's [Henry World Cup profile](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/exclusive-world-cup-club-12-years-champions) identifies him with France's 1998 World Cup victory.
- Arsenal's [Bergkamp anniversary feature](https://www.arsenal.com/news/30-years-dennis-bergkamp-signs-arsenal?id=14333) gives 423 Arsenal appearances and 120 goals across the 1995–2006 club career.
- The Premier League's [Bergkamp history](https://www.premierleague.com/es/news/759819) confirms his 1995 arrival from Inter, Dutch identity, retirement at the end of 2005/06, and league-only total of 87 goals in 315 appearances.

## Editorial checks and limitations

Henry's 377 appearances and 228 goals are whole-Arsenal-career totals, while 258 appearances and 175 goals are Premier League-only totals. An older Premier League retrospective describes his 1999–2007 first spell as 254 league appearances, 174 league goals and 226 all-competition goals, then says the 2012 loan added one league goal. The later Premier League profile gives the final Premier League total of 258/175; Arsenal's career feature gives the complete two-spell totals of 377/228. The lesson uses the later cumulative league figures and complete Arsenal totals, with scopes stated in the story and facts. An older Arsenal statue page also gives 226 goals, matching the pre-return count; the current club career feature's 228 figure is used for the complete career.

The Arsenal article pages were discoverable as official Arsenal sources, but Arsenal.com returned 403 to the browser fetcher; the official Shorthand-hosted Henry feature opened and was readable. The official Arsenal Bergkamp page was independently surfaced with its full article text in search results, while direct fetch returned 403. The Premier League articles opened successfully and independently support the league-only counts and career milestones. The Arsenal matchday-programme PDF with an appearance table returned 410, so it was not used as the source of the 377 Henry total; the readable Arsenal-hosted Henry career feature supports it directly.
