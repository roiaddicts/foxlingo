## Uncertainties and scope limits

- Del Piero’s goal total is intentionally omitted: Juventus’s contemporary retrospective gives 289 goals in 705 matches, while the Hall of Fame profile shows 290. The pack uses the shared appearance total and other non-conflicting profile records.
- Buffon’s 648 Serie A appearance record is dated 4 July 2020. The source separately gives 480 Serie A appearances for Juventus; the pack keeps those club-only and all-time figures distinct.
- Napoli’s 1 August 1926 date is labeled the club’s symbolic founding date. Its official account separately says the 25 August meeting adopted the name Associazione Calcio Napoli; the two dates are not conflated.
- Hamšík’s 121 goals are supported by UEFA’s dated October 2019 all-time list. The separate Napoli retrospective supports 520 appearances and 12 seasons; the UEFA 2018 profile is not used as proof of his later final total.
- Inter’s 2010 treble lesson is also the setting for Zanetti’s and Milito’s player lessons, but the player facts are specific to their own role or match contribution; club history lessons focus on 1964 and 1965 instead.
