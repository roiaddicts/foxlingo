## Source limitations and scope

- The 2013 Apertura facts use Chivas’ readable official article; the 2014 return-leg facts use its official archive article URL. The 2014 archive record is available through the official indexed excerpt but is not consistently readable in the research reader, so those facts remain flagged as archive/index-limited.
- The opened FC Barcelona club profile is an opponent-club source for León’s return and bicampeonato chronology; it supports the club-level retrospective, while Concacaf is used for the 2023 continental run and individual tournament records.
- Dávila’s 25 goals in 77 León matches is a dated snapshot from Concacaf’s February 27, 2023 preview, not a current career total. All 2023 SCCL player totals are scoped to that competition edition.
- Ambriz’s first-team appearance snapshot comes from Concacaf’s pre-2023 tournament profile; the award and youngest-scorer claims come from its official 2023 award report. His academy arrival and debut ages are from a Club León quote reproduced by Concacaf.
- Cruz Azul’s own historical pages and Pumas’ official history, palmarès, chronology and player biography support club milestones. Where a fact concerns a club’s own past, its scope is stated as season, tournament, or final to avoid presenting an era-specific figure as a live statistic.
- Some lesson story screens summarize their own associated facts to keep the two-screen path coherent; they do not introduce additional unsourced statistics.
