# Competition curriculum notes

Nine foundation lessons, three per competition, introduce 27 distinct memories. Each has two story pages and four retrieval questions. The fourth question recalls the first anchor again; it does not create another memory. Existing club lessons are optional context, with their original canonical IDs.

## Verification and scope

Reviewed on 2026-10-08 using built-in browsing after the Firecrawl CLI was unavailable. UEFA and Bundesliga sources are official competition histories and season reports. Chivas and Santos histories are official club sources. El Informador is an on-site report of the 2012 Liga MX presentation; Claro Sports supplies the historical short-tournament overview. These two journalism sources are not labeled federation publications.

The initial draft’s unverifiable fmf.com.mx archive citations were removed, along with the claims that depended on them. Mexico’s professional origins (1943/44) are distinct from the Liga MX identity (2012). The course teaches two tournament cycles and their historical names without claiming every calendar year has two completed championships. Special names and cancelled editions are acknowledged.

The European Cup’s first group stage was in 1991/92; the Champions League name followed in 1992/93. Format facts are scoped to the 2024/25 introduction. Bundesliga origins refer to West Germany; reunification explains the one-off 20-team season. Leverkusen statistics belong to the completed 2023/24 league season.

Each fact retains its supporting source, evidence and scope in competition-lessons.json. No live standings, current valuations or changing all-time totals are introduced.

## Artwork

Liga MX’s CDN returned HTTP 403, so its mark uses the unmodified Commons file credited to Hectortoro2019 under CC BY-SA 4.0. These are cached offline. Logos remain third-party competition marks.

## Model follow-up

The current compiler uses the competition as the fact subject and keeps person/club context in each claim’s scope. Borgetti’s personal goal count therefore cannot yet be discovered by a canonical Borgetti player subject. Future player expansion should separate curriculum ownership from the fact subject and add sourced player/event relationships; it must preserve the existing objective identity or explicitly revise its meaning.
