## QA and limits

The 22 lessons divide into three Köln club lessons, three Schalke club lessons, and four lessons each for Podolski, Modeste, Huntelaar, and Farfan. Each lesson has two story screens and three facts. The pack has 66 unique fact keys, three answer choices per fact, and every fact’s source key resolves to the pack’s source list. Player lesson chapters are distinct within each path: career, two separate moments, and records (or honours for the club paths). The Schalke 1984 score is clearly a first-match score after extra time; the replay result is separately scoped.

The Modeste Hamburg scoring attribution is supported by the Bundesliga’s historical match preview, while DFB supplies the exact date and score. DFB’s Datencenter is the source for the season appearance/match list; its search-visible page is less readable than the club and league articles, so the two-hat-trick count is the least transparent claim in the pack. If the parent’s browser cannot inspect the Datencenter match list, drop that single record fact rather than infer a total from a search result. The DFB Modeste profile used here is the 2016/17 season profile, not a career-total snapshot.

No facts were added for current or changing stats. The Schalke 04 club key is `entity-schalke-04`, matching the expansion’s canonical club slug. No compilation or registry edits were made.
