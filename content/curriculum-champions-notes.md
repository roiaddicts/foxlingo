## Scope notes

- Player additions use completed historical events or totals. They do not describe current squads or changing records.
- The 91-goal Messi fact is the calendar-year total across club and country; separate facts state 79 Barça and 12 Argentina goals.
- Henry’s 30-goal number is Premier League-only. The separate hat-trick lesson is a specific all-competition match event.
- The 1984 Liverpool score refers to the penalty shoot-out after a 1–1 match; the shoot-out result is 4–2.
- Existing starter curriculum already covers Arsenal’s unbeaten league season, Liverpool’s 2005 Istanbul final, and earlier career/record introductions for all six players. These additions use different events and questions.
- The source-backed date for the Bergkamp-Newcastle match is 2 March 2002 and the source identifies St James’ Park and Newcastle. No final score is asked for that Bergkamp match.
