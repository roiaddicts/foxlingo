# Premier League foundation course

Verified 2026-10-09 using four official Premier League pages: league history, the 1992/93 review, the 2003/04 review and the 2021 Shearer/Henry Hall of Fame announcement. Three required lessons teach nine distinct scoped memories; an existing Arsenal history lesson is optional. The new competition course does not declare a current Premier League club membership list.

1992 is the competition’s establishment year, not the beginning of English league football. The inaugural season had 22 clubs and Manchester United won the title. Arsenal’s 2003/04 unbeaten record is league-only: 26 wins and 12 draws, not an undefeated record across all competitions or the longer 49-game league run. Shearer’s 260 counts his complete Premier League career; Henry’s 30 goals is the single-season 2003/04 league count and is distinct from his all-competition club total. Four Golden Boots counts awards, not goals. The records are supported by the historical 2021 induction announcement and do not imply live 2026 leaderboards.

Artwork uses the 196-pixel competition icon linked by the official league site. The original is cached as PNG; the asset provenance is recorded in competition-artwork.json. Existing IDs and required club lessons remain unchanged.
