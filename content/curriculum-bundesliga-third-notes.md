## Statistical reconciliation

The English HSV tribute labels 90 appearances and 32 goals as competitive totals. DFB’s directly opened competition rows establish these as Bundesliga-only totals, independently corroborated by HSV’s German season table. The lessons use the league scope and cite DFB; no unreconciled all-competition total is taught.
