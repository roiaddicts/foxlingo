## Evidence

- 1966–67: [official trophy page](https://www.tolucafc.com/trofeo-1966-1967). It identifies América as level on points with three matches left, three consecutive Toluca victories and Juan Dosal’s two goals against Necaxa. The page supports those three facts directly. It does not explicitly state Necaxa’s goal total, so no inferred score is authored. The story’s fourteen-year wait is also explicit.
- 1974–75: [official trophy page](https://www.tolucafc.com/trofeo-1974-1975). It describes a four-club double round-robin final stage, the 1–0 home win over León, Ítalo Estupiñán’s goal and eight points with a fixture pending. The score is a single match, not an aggregate; the points are at clinching, not a final-season total. Toluca and León have existing cached badges.

The 1967–68 and Verano 1999 pages were also inspected but were not used to add redundant title-year or shootout-save questions. All prompts name their campaign and context. No current league totals or squads are asserted.

- Verano 2000: [official trophy page](https://www.tolucafc.com/palmares-verano-2000), directly opened. It names Enrique Meza, a 2–0 away first leg and 7–1 aggregate against Santos. Those are distinct score scopes; no return-leg result or unspecified regular-season record is inferred.
- Apertura 2005: [official trophy page](https://www.tolucafc.com/palmares-apertura-2005), directly opened. It states 3–3 at home and 3–0 away against Monterrey and singles out Vicente Sánchez. No unsupported personal goal count is added. The club explicitly describes this as its first championship clinched away from home, not its first away victory.

The guessed Bicentenario 2010 URL was inaccessible; no claims from it enter this pack. All four lessons retain two short story screens and three distinct facts. Score wheels consistently show Toluca on the left, including away matches.
