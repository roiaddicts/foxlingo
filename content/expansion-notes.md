# Football club expansion content notes

Verified on 2026-10-08. The catalog contains **68 unique clubs** across the requested scopes: 36 in the 2026/27 UEFA Champions League league phase, 18 in the 2026/27 Bundesliga, and 18 in Liga MX Apertura 2026. Four clubs appear in both the Champions League and Bundesliga. Real Madrid (`madrid`) and Club Tijuana (`xolos`) remain catalog entries with `lessons: []`; the other 66 clubs each have one starter lesson, two story pages, and three source-keyed fact prompts.

Membership was checked against UEFA’s 2026/27 team-by-team league-phase preview, the Bundesliga’s official 2026/27 club overview, and Atlas FC’s official Apertura 2026 schedule. Atlas’s page lists the home club and its 17 distinct opponents; the league-calendar announcement also confirms an 18-club tournament. The Atlas schedule supplies competition membership and badge assets only. It is not used for club-learning quiz facts. The official 2026/27 sources provide the requested membership snapshots as of the verification date; later changes or corrections are not covered.

The 35 Champions League lessons use UEFA’s qualification route, country, and best European Cup performance, and the story pages state those assessed answers. The 14 Bundesliga-only starters keep a league-membership fact and a listed home ground, while replacing the generic country question with a sourced founding date, title, or historical milestone. Their two story pages teach the league, ground, and historical answer. The four clubs also in the Champions League retain their UEFA starter lesson, which includes a sourced European history fact.

The 17 additional Liga MX lessons cover club origins, colors, names, stadiums, and historical moments using official club pages, an official club-history PDF, or FIFA’s official Club León profile. Every lesson’s two story pages state the answers assessed by its three facts. Club Tijuana is excluded from new lesson content as requested, but remains a Liga MX catalog entry. Atlas’s schedule and N+’s calendar report are retained as competition-membership provenance, not quiz sources.

`logoURL` reuses UEFA badge URLs already present in the project seed data when available. Liga MX opponent badge assets exposed by Atlas FC’s official schedule are used. Direct logo URLs are `null` for 31 catalog entries where neither the reviewed seed data nor an official page exposed a reusable URL; those were left blank instead of guessed. Some seed badge URLs are hosted by UEFA or the project’s existing image host, as in the source seed.

The JSON was parsed and checked for unique club keys and source keys, three distinct distractors per fact, answers excluded from distractors, source keys resolving to the `sources` list, and two story pages per lesson. Exact counts: **68 clubs, 66 starter lessons, and 198 fact prompts**. Membership totals: **36 Champions League, 18 Bundesliga, and 18 Liga MX**.

Integration review gave every matching label a distinct meaning and shortened European-history questions to a first-title season or best finish, rather than requiring a full trophy list. Canonical Atlético de Madrid relationships reuse the original entity ID. `club-artwork.json` resolves all 68 badge sources, including Wikipedia infobox fallbacks where the original draft had no URL.
